# antislop audit 001: Streamly (ott-client), 2026-11-17

Mode: AFTER. Nothing has been changed. Pick the numbers to fix; numbers not mentioned are not touched.

Scope: the public stream pages (Home, Movies, Series, Reality, Show all, title details, player, pricing, account,
paywall), the navbar and footer, and the error pages. Admin and the kit's settings pages are out of scope.

Direction: there is no `DESIGN.md`. The design direction lives in `CLAUDE.md` ("Design rules" and "Motion rules":
dark theme on stream pages, glass panels, grain, accent gradient, display type, scroll motion on every section).
That counts as the owner's direction (R-37). Where the direction itself asks for a pattern antislop names, the
finding says so and asks you to keep or drop it instead of treating it as slop.

Evidence: code inspection of `ott-client/src` and `ott-server/src/config/plans.js`, plus a live run of the dev site
at 375×812 (mobile, touch) with scripted checks for overflow, tap targets, titles and images, and colour pairs
measured with the antislop contrast checker.

Priority follows the rule tier: Hard Gate = HIGH, Purpose-Gate = MEDIUM, Quality Locks = LOW.

## HIGH (Hard Gate)

1. **R-24 / R-26: navigation to pages that don't exist.** The navbar's My List link and search icon, the footer's
   Search link and the profile menu's My List all open the 404 page (`/my-list` and `/search` have no route).
2. **R-36 / R-38: pricing features without evidence.** `plans.js` lists "Full HD streaming", "Watch on 2 screens"
   (Monthly), "Watch on 4 screens" and "2 months free" (Yearly). No screen limit exists in the code, no Premium
   title has video yet, and Yearly saves ₹389 while two months cost ₹398, so "2 months free" overstates it.
3. **R-38 / C-5: taking payments with no Terms of Service, Privacy Policy or refund and cancellation policy.**
   Pricing and checkout are live; no legal page exists and the footer links none. (Razorpay also asks for these.)
4. **C-5 / R-38: TMDB data and images shown without the attribution TMDB requires.** The footer only has a TODO.
5. **R-39: every page has the same `<title>` ("Streamly").** Measured on Home, Movies, Reality, Pricing, a title
   page and Show all. Screen-reader users and browser tabs can't tell pages apart.
6. **R-39: the hero carousel moves for longer than 5 seconds with no pause control.** `FilmstripHero` auto-advances
   every 7 s and pauses only on hover, focus or scrolling away; there is no visible pause button. (Reduced motion
   already turns autoplay off.)
7. **R-39: a control whose name doesn't match its visible text.** The active Coverflow card is announced as "Watch
   … trailer" while showing the title (Lighthouse `label-content-name-mismatch`). Voice-control users can't say it.
8. **R-03: tap targets under 44 px on phones (375 px measured).** Navbar Search and menu buttons 40×40, FilterBar
   dropdowns 40 px tall, sort switch 32 px, season tabs 32 px, pricing Monthly/Yearly 40 px, "Show all" 40 px, the
   mood band's "Browse …" 36 px, 404 page buttons 36 px, skip link 36 px.
9. **R-02: em dash in UI text.** The Account page shows "—" for a missing date and a missing payment method.
10. **R-25: text over images not verified at the worst spot.** Hero title and copy, landscape card titles, the
    details hero and the player's top bar sit on photos or video behind scrims. The scrims look right, but the
    worst-case contrast was not measured, so this is unverified, not passed.
11. **R-23 / R-39: the sign-in and sign-up pages show the starter kit's logo,** whose accessible name is
    "Shadcn-Admin". A screen reader reads the wrong product name, and the logo was never confirmed.
12. **R-39: the pointer-cursor base rule covers only `button` and `[role=button]`.** `select`, `summary` and
    `label[for]` keep the arrow cursor. (Links and Radix triggers are fine.)

## MEDIUM (Purpose-Gate: technique allowed, reason missing)

13. **R-10, direction conflict: glass well past the 1-2 element dose.** Home has 63 `glass`/`glass-strong` elements
    and 71 elements with `backdrop-filter` (navbar, cards, pills, filter dropdowns, preview, panels, dialogs,
    buttons). `CLAUDE.md` asks for "glass panels", so: keep glass everywhere, or keep it on 1-2 surfaces (navbar and
    the hover preview, say) and make the rest solid?
14. **R-13: glows on most bands.** A radial glow behind each page header and section band, plus the hero
    atmosphere, with no written reason for each. Keep one or two where they mark the focal point?
15. **R-08: arrow icons on 25 links and buttons** ("Show all", pricing and account CTAs, "Start exploring", hero
    and carousel controls). Keep the arrow only where it signals "go somewhere", with that reason written down.
16. **R-04: every icon comes from Lucide (32 files),** with no written reason for the library look.
17. **R-06, direction conflict: 52 uppercase, wide-tracked eyebrow labels on Home** (STORY, PLAN, BROWSE, numbered
    band eyebrows). `CLAUDE.md` defines `text-eyebrow`, so: keep it as the identity motif (and write the reason), or
    reduce it to section bands only?
18. **R-09 / R-31: decorative dots that mark no state.** Ten accent-gradient dots, for example before "SERIES" in
    the details hero and inside the "Everything" badge on pricing. The "Active" dot on the account page marks a
    real state and is fine.
19. **R-19, direction conflict: a fade-up reveal on every section and card.** `CLAUDE.md` says "every section gets
    scroll motion", which is the "template animations" tell. Keep it as the declared MOTION 3 dial, or keep motion
    for the hero, the Top 10 and the preview and let the rest appear still?

## LOW (Quality Locks)

20. **R-15: generic CTA "Start exploring"** on the corridor band. Something specific, for example "Browse all 720
    titles", would say what happens.
21. **R-16 / C-1: the 404 and error pages are the kit's,** with generic copy ("Oops! Page Not Found!", "It seems
    like the page you're looking for…"), the kit's styling and no navbar or footer.
22. **C-3: dev and demo pages reachable in production:** `/lab`, `/lab/next`, `/lab/components` and the kit's
    `/dashboard`.
23. **R-31 / R-21: no written one-line reasons for the main visual decisions.** Dark-only, grain, glass, the
    eyebrow style and the type pairing are stated in `CLAUDE.md` but not justified. A short `DESIGN.md` with one
    line per decision (for example "dark only: video looks best on dark, like a cinema") would close this.

## Checked and passing

- No horizontal overflow at 375 px on Home, Movies, Reality, Pricing, a title page, Show all and the 404 page.
- Text tokens on the stream background: body 18.46:1, muted 8.69:1, muted on hover surfaces 7.26:1, secondary text
  on muted surfaces 7.8:1 (all pass AA).
- Hero image is an `<img>` with `fetchpriority="high"`, not lazy. CLS measured 0.00 on Home.
- No invented statistics, testimonials or FAQ; the catalogue count on Home is real data.
- Pricing is two cards (Free and Premium), not three tiers, with no "most popular" badge.
- Rails, grids, title details, player and billing have loading, empty and error states with a next action.
- Focus rings use `focus-visible` with a visible ring; Escape closes the title modal, paywall and player panels.
- Reduced motion turns off autoplay, pinning and movement (verified in earlier phases).

## Not measured

- A Lighthouse performance score for Home. Only a throttled trace was taken (first paint 9.6 s under 4× CPU and slow
  4G on an uncompressed local server); see the final-pass preview.
- Worst-spot contrast of text over images (finding 10).
